# Plating Card: Halibut Ceviche Tostada

**DRAFT. Illustrative documentation example.**

## Plate

Large cold plate. Three tostadas in a line or shallow arc, evenly spaced.

## Build Order

1. Three fried tostadas on the plate.
2. Beet aioli drizzled on each tostada. This is structural, not garnish. It anchors the slaw and keeps the tostada from drying out under the load.
3. Slaw dressed to order, placed on each tostada.
4. Ceviche ladled over the top. 110 g total across the three tostadas, distributed evenly. Include liquid with each ladle. The mix without its liquid is a different dish.

## Quality Check

- Tostadas rigid at the pass. A soft tostada does not leave the kitchen.
- Ceviche cold, below 4 C. Verify by touch on the ladle, thermometer on the batch every service.
- Halibut dice intact and opaque. Broken or ragged fish means the fold was rough or the cure ran long.
- Color check: red onion and tomato visible through the mix, beet aioli edge showing under the slaw.
- No drips on the rim. Wipe before it moves.

## Service Window

Immediate. This plate has a countdown from the moment ceviche hits slaw. Target under 60 seconds from ladle to table. Expedite accordingly.

## Time

- Plating time: under 90 seconds.
- Hold time: none. Fire on pickup only.
