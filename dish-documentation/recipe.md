# Halibut Ceviche Tostada

**DRAFT. Illustrative documentation example.**

Three proteins, three treatments, one cold plate. Halibut tamari marinated and citrus cured, prawns boiled in Old Bay and lemon, bay scallops acid cured with Aleppo. The mix rides a tomato base that pulls the dish toward a Veracruz build rather than a straight Peruvian. Served over three fried corn tortillas with beet aioli and lemon dressed slaw.

Tamari note: the marinade runs tamari, not soy sauce. Same fermented soy depth, no wheat, and with corn tortillas underneath that makes the whole plate gluten free as built. Sub standard soy sauce back in and the dish stops being GF; if you do that, update the server reference the same day.

Halibut note: halibut is leaner and more delicate than the firm tropical fish this build was first developed around. It takes acid faster, and it goes chalky if you overshoot the cure. Cure times below are written for halibut. Respect them.

## Method

### Halibut
1. Cut halibut into 12 mm dice. Keep the knife sharp and the fish cold. Halibut flakes apart under a dull blade.
2. Toss with tamari. Marinate 10 minutes, no longer. Lean flesh takes salt fast.
3. Drain. Transfer to the orange and lemon juice bath. Cure 25 to 35 minutes, checking at 25. Pull when opaque through and just firm. Past 45 minutes halibut turns chalky. Drain and hold cold, separate.

### Prawns
1. Bring water to a boil with Old Bay and lemon.
2. Add prawns. Boil until just cooked through. Pull immediately.
3. Cool, peel, chop small. Chill.

### Bay Scallops
1. Cut scallops small.
2. Combine with lime juice, lemon juice, and Aleppo pepper.
3. Cure separately until firm, 20 to 30 minutes. Hold cold.

### Ceviche Mix
1. Combine roma tomato, red onion, garlic, pickled jalapeños and brine, champagne vinegar, cocktail sauce, tomato juice, and sambal oelek. Season with salt and pepper.
2. Fold in cured halibut and chilled prawns. Fold gently. The halibut dice must stay intact.
3. Fold in scallops last. Rest the full batch cold at least 1 hour to marry before service.

### Tostadas
1. Fry yellow corn tortillas in batches until rigid and blistered. Season immediately out of the oil.
2. Hold dry for service. Three per plate.

### Slaw
1. Dress coleslaw mix with lemon juice and pickled red onion to order. Never pre-dress.

### Beet Aioli
1. Mix beet juice stock into miso mayo until incorporated. Adjust ratio for color and flavor. Hold in squeeze bottle, refrigerated.

## Critical Points

- Cure discipline. The halibut cure window is the make or break step. Opaque and just firm is done. Firm and springy is overdone.
- Cold chain. Every component of this dish lives at or below 4 C from prep through plating. No exceptions.
- Fold order. Halibut and prawns first, scallops last. Scallops are the most fragile texture on the plate.
- Speed at the pass. Tostadas soften fast once slaw and ceviche land. Plate, wipe, move.
- The batch rests before service. Day of prep, same day service. This mix does not hold to day two.
